Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate pricing?

Our rates are flat-rate based on room counts, service type, and frequency. No surprise hourly charges.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most clients leave lockbox, smart keypad, or concierge instructions. Cleaners are bonded and insured.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes! We provide hospital-grade HEPA vacuums, microfiber cloths, and plant-based eco-friendly cleaners.

What if I'm not satisfied?

Contact us within 24 hours and we return to re-clean any area free of charge.

How far in advance should I book?

2 to 3 days is recommended. Same-day emergency bookings are also accommodated whenever possible.
